How Partner Recommendations Work

  • AI Analysis: System analyzes your project requirements against partner product capabilities
  • Fit Scoring: Each product gets a compatibility score (0-100%) based on technical and business fit
  • Integration Scope: Core (essential), Add-on (valuable), Optional (nice-to-have)
  • Cost Integration: Selected partner costs are included in final proposal pricing

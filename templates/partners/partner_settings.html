About Partner Management

Partner management enables AI-powered cross-sell recommendations during proposal generation. The system analyzes project requirements and suggests relevant partner products that complement your solutions.

Benefits:
  • Automated product recommendations
  • Enhanced proposal value
  • Revenue sharing opportunities
Partner Types:
  • Strategic: Long-term partnerships
  • Technology: Software/hardware providers
  • Integration: System specialists
